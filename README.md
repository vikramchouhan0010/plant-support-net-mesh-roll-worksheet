# Plant support net: mesh and roll size worksheet

Plant support net is a wide-mesh net stretched upright or flat above a bed so climbing and spreading crops grow through it. Two choices decide what you order: the mesh opening and the roll size.

## Roll sizes listed in the BLUSTAL catalogue

| Mesh | Roll size | Area per roll |
|---|---|---|
| 4 inch | 0.90 m x 100 m | 90 sq m |
| 5 inch | 1.25 m x 50 m | 62.5 sq m |
| 6 inch | 1.50 m x 30 m | 45 sq m |
| 8 inch | 2 m x 50 m | 100 sq m |
| 10 inch | 2 m x 50 m | 100 sq m |

Listed in black and blue.

## Worksheet
1. Count the rows and write the length of each row.
2. Decide whether the net runs upright along the row or flat above the bed. That sets the width you need.
3. Divide the total run you need by the roll length and round up. Example: 600 m of row at 100 m per roll is 6 rolls of the 4 inch net.
4. Add a margin for overlaps and end fixing.
5. Check posts, wire and clips are in the same enquiry, not ordered separately later.

## Send with the enquiry
Crop, row count and length, upright or flat, mesh you prefer, colour, quantity of rolls.
